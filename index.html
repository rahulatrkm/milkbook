A closed month cannot be changed by a stray tap, on any phone sharing this book. You can always unlock one when you genuinely need to.

Closing months protects the ones that have ended, but the month on screen is the one a thumb actually brushes. While it is protected, tapping a day does nothing and today is marked with the button at the bottom — a long press there sets a different quantity.

Opening a month is for making a correction, not for leaving it open. When the time is up it protects or closes itself again, and the chip above the calendar hands it back the moment you are done.

Turns off every lock at once, for whoever keeps the book. Nothing is hidden and nothing is deleted — it only stops the book refusing you. A banner stays on screen while it is on, so it is never on by accident.

Pick a file inside your Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox folder and the book reaches the cloud on its own, without this app ever touching your account. Choose allow on every visit when the browser asks, so it keeps working without being told again.

Keeps one file in your own Drive and reads it back when you open the app, so a new phone only has to sign in. This app can only touch the file it made — nothing else in your Drive — and never sees your password.

Your records live on your phones and nowhere else — the sharing code is a meeting point, not storage. A copy kept somewhere else is the one thing that survives a phone being lost, wiped or replaced. Restoring adds a copy back in without removing anything already here.
